§4.4 II-3（预习 p1）成环条件与两镜位置

Give the conditions of generating the Michelson interference rings at the frosted glass screen and
describe the relationship between the positions of the moving mirror and the fixed mirror.
中文理解。条件：两束光相干并在屏上重叠；等效膜的两面平行（M1 ∥ M2′ ）；屏与两虚源连线 S1 S2′

English short answer.The two returned beams must be coherent and overlap on the frosted screen;

the coherence length, and the ring centre must be on the screen. M2′ is the virtual image of M2 in
G1 , and d = |M1 M2′ |; a movement of M2 changes d by the same amount. Parallel M1 and M2′ give
circular fringes; a small tilt gives straight, elliptical or hyperbolic fringes.

§4.5 II-6（预习 p1）把小半径环调成大半径环

中文理解。对某一固定级次 k，有 2d cos θk = kλ。d 增大时 cos θk 减小，θk 增大，这一圈环向外扩；

so test it first.

§4.6 II-7（预习 p1）为什么环会吐或吞

中文理解。中心 θ = 0，所以 2d = kλ。d 增大，中心级次 k0 = 2d/λ 增大；每增一级，就有一个新环

fixed, so that ring moves outward.

§4.10 预习 p3 报告问题 (2) 没有补偿板为什么没有白光条纹

Why can’t a white interference fringe be obtained without a compensation plate?
中文理解。白光包含多种波长；玻璃有色散，不同波长的光穿过同样厚度玻璃产生的光程不同。没有
G2 时，两束光穿过的玻璃厚度不同，各波长的零级不在同一位置，彩色条纹互相错开、叠加成一片白，

                                                 _ 14 _
实验 3.11 原理与预习节选（v2 试读）                                                                              大物实验


所以看不到白光条纹。G2 与 G1 同材料、同厚度并平行，使各波长的玻璃路程相同，零级重合，才出
现白光中心纹和两侧彩纹。
English short answer.White light contains many wavelengths. Glass is dispersive, so the optical
path through G1 depends on wavelength. Without G2 , the two beams pass different thicknesses of
glass, the zero-order condition occurs at different positions for different colours, and the coloured
fringes overlap into a uniform white field. G2 , made of the same material and thickness as G1 and
parallel to it, equalizes the glass path for all wavelengths, so the zero-order fringes coincide and a
white central fringe with coloured sides can be seen.
