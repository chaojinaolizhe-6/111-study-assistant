### 2.4 Series AC circuits

### Examples

E1 Series AC circuit , try to find R and X in the following cases.

①

$$
Z _ {1} = 3 0 0 + \mathrm{j} 5 0 0
$$

②

$$
\dot {U} = 50\angle 30^\circ\text{V}
$$

$$
I = 2 \angle 30^\circ \text{A}
$$

③

$$
\begin{array}{l} \dot {\boldsymbol {U}} = 1 0 0 \angle - 3 0 ^ {\circ} \mathrm{V} \\ \dot {\boldsymbol {I}} = 4 \angle 4 0 ^ {\circ} \mathrm{A} \end{array}
$$

Effective expression

$$
U = \left| Z \right| I
$$
