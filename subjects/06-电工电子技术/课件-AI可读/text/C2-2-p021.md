E4 Given that $U = 2 2 0 ~ \mathrm { V } , ~ R _ { 1 } = 2 0 ~ \Omega ~ , R _ { 2 } = 4 0 ~ \Omega , X _ { C } = 1 1 4 ~ \Omega ,$ $X _ { L } = 1 5 7 \Omega ,$ Find overall I.

Solution 1: Using Branch current

$$
Z_1 = R_1 - jX_C = 20 - j114 = 116\angle -80^\circ \Omega
$$

$$
Z _ {2} = R _ {2} + j X _ {L} = 4 0 + j 1 5 7 = 1 6 2 \angle 7 5. 7 ^ {\circ} \Omega
$$

$$
\dot {I} _ {1} = \frac {\dot {U}}{Z _ {1}} = \frac {2 2 0}{1 1 6 \angle - 8 0 ^ {\circ}} = 1. 9 \angle 8 0 ^ {\circ} \mathrm{A}
$$

（此位置版面为图，见整页扫描图）

$$
\dot {I} _ {2} = \frac {U}{Z _ {2}} = \frac {2 2 0}{1 6 2 \angle - 7 5 . 7 ^ {\circ}} = 1. 3 6 \angle - 7 5. 7 ^ {\circ} \mathrm{A}
$$

$$
\dot {I} = \dot {I} _ {1} + \dot {I} _ {2} = 0. 8 6 2 \angle 3 9. 6 ^ {\circ} \mathrm{A}
$$
