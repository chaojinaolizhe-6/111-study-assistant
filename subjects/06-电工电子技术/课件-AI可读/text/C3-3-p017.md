### 1. Zener diode vs Diode

（此位置版面为图，见整页扫描图）

### Characteristic：

① Reverse breakdown voltage is small.

② Reverse breakdown characteristic is steep.

### Applications:

❶ It is normally used as voltage reference; ❷ Zener diodes are used in voltage stabilizers or shunt regulators; ❸ Zener diodes are used in switching operations;

❹ Zener diodes are used in clipping and clamping circuits; 削波和箝位电路 ❺ Zener diodes are used in various protection circuits.
