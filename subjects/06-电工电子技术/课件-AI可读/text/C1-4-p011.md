### Notice:

(1) It is for lineal circuit . In order to use the superposition theorem with circuit currents and voltages, all of the components must be linear

(2) It is not applicable to power. Since power is not a linear quantity. The total power delivered to a resistive component must be determined using the total current through or the total voltage across the component and cannot be determined by a simple sum of the powers produced by the sources independently.

(3)Pay attention to the direction. It is not applicable to power, since power is not a linear quantity. The total power delivered to a resistive component must be determined using the total current through or the total voltage across the component and cannot be determined by a simple sum of the powers produced by the sources independently.

(4) Each group can have more than one sources.
