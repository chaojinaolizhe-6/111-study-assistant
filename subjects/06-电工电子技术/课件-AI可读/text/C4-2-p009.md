### ❸ Maximum collector current $I _ { \mathbf { C M } }$

When the collector current exceeds a certain value, the parameters of the transistor begin to change, especially the current gain will decrease. When current gain beta drops to two-thirds of its normal value, the corresponding collector current is called collector maximum allowable current.
