E4 Given that $U = 2 2 0 ~ \mathrm { V } , ~ R _ { 1 } = 2 0 ~ \Omega ~ , R _ { 2 } = 4 0 ~ \Omega , X _ { C } = 1 1 4 ~ \Omega ,$ $X _ { L } = 1 5 7 \Omega ,$ Find overall I.

Solution 2: Using total impedance

$$
Z _ {1} = R _ {1} - j X _ {C} = 2 0 - j 1 1 4 = 1 1 6 \angle - 8 0 ^ {\circ} \Omega
$$

$$
Z _ {2} = R _ {2} + j X _ {L} = 4 0 + j 1 5 7 = 1 6 2 \angle 7 5. 7 ^ {\circ} \Omega
$$

$$
\mathrm{Z} = \frac {\mathrm{Z} _ {1} \cdot \mathrm{Z} _ {2}}{\mathrm{Z} _ {1} + \mathrm{Z} _ {2}} = 2 5 5 \angle - 3 9. 6 ^ {\circ} \Omega
$$

$$
\begin{array}{l} \dot {U} = 2 2 0 \angle 0 ^ {\circ} \mathrm{V} \\ \dot {I} = \frac {\dot {U}}{Z} = \frac {2 2 0}{2 5 5 \angle - 3 9 . 6 ^ {\circ}} = 0. 8 6 2 \angle 3 9. 6 ^ {\circ} \mathrm{A} \end{array}
$$
