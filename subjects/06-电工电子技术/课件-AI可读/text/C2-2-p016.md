### 2.5 Parallel AC circuits

### 2.5.2 Parallel impedance circuit

◎Resistors connected in parallel

$$
R = \frac {R _ {1} \cdot R _ {2}}{R _ {1} + R _ {2}}
$$

$$
I _ {R _ {1}} = \frac {R _ {2}}{R _ {1} + R _ {2}} I
$$

$$
I = \frac {U}{\frac {R _ {1} \cdot R _ {2}}{R _ {1} + R _ {2}}}
$$

$$
I _ {R _ {2}} = \frac {R _ {1}}{R _ {1} + R _ {2}} I
$$

◎Impedance connected in parallel

$$
Z = \frac{Z_1 \cdot Z_2}{Z_1 + Z_2}
$$

$$
\dot {I} _ {1} = \frac {Z _ {2}}{Z _ {1} + Z _ {2}} \dot {I}
$$

$$
\dot{I} = \frac{\dot{U}}{Z}
$$

$$
\dot {I} _ {2} = \frac {Z _ {1}}{Z _ {1} + Z _ {2}} \dot {I}
$$
