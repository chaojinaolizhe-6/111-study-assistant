### 2.4.1 Series RLC circuits

❷ Phasor expression and effective expression

$$
\dot {\boldsymbol {U}} = \left\lbrace \boldsymbol {R} + \mathbf {j} \left(\boldsymbol {X} _ {L} - \boldsymbol {X} _ {C}\right) \right\rbrace
$$

$$
\dot {\boldsymbol {U}} = \boldsymbol {Z} \dot {\boldsymbol {I}}
$$

Ohms law in phasor form

$$
\dot {\boldsymbol {U}} = \boldsymbol {Z} \dot {\boldsymbol {I}}
$$

Phasor expression

$$
\boldsymbol {U} = \left| \boldsymbol {Z} \right| \boldsymbol {I}
$$

Effective expression

$$
\begin{array}{c} \text {+} \quad \text {i} \\ \text {R} \quad \text {+} \quad \text {u} _ {R} \\ \text {u} \quad \text {L} \quad \text {+} \quad \text {u} _ {L} \\ \text {C} \quad \text {+} \quad \text {u} _ {C} \\ \text {-} \end{array}
$$

Two kinds of information, including magnitude and phase.

Only the information of magnitude. It is used when we only need to calculate the magnitude.
