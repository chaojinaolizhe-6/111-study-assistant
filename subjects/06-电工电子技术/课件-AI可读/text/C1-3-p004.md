### Gustav Robert Kirchhoff Robert

（此位置版面为图，见整页扫描图）

Gustav Robert Kirchhoff was a German physicist. He is best known for developing Kirchhoff's laws, which quantify the current and voltage in electrical circuits. In addition to Kirchhoff's laws, Kirchhoff made a number of other fundamental contributions to physics, including work on spectroscopy and blackbody radiation.

Kirchhoff's laws can be used for the analysis of DC circuits, AC circuits and nonlinear circuits with electronic components.
