### 2.2.1 Phasor representation of sine wave

❷ Basic operations with complex numbers

Supposed： $A _ { 1 } = a _ { 1 } + \mathbf { j } \ b _ { 1 } = c _ { 1 } / \psi _ { 1 }$

$$
A _ {2} = a _ {2} + \mathrm{j} b _ {2} = c _ {2} \angle \psi_ {2} \neq 0
$$

Addition, subtraction：

$$
A _ {1} \pm A _ {2} = (a _ {1} \pm a _ {2}) + \mathbf {j} (b _ {1} \pm b _ {2})
$$

Multiplication:

$$
A _ {1} A _ {2} = c _ {1} c _ {2} \angle \psi_ {1} + \psi_ {2}
$$

Division：

$$
\frac {A _ {1}}{A _ {2}} = \frac {c _ {1}}{c _ {2}} \angle \psi_ {1} - \psi_ {2}
$$
