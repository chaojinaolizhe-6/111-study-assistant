### Ⅰ Concept：

（此位置版面为图，见整页扫描图）

(1) What is Intrinsic semiconductor?

(2) What are semiconductor materials?

An intrinsic semiconductor is a pure semiconductor without any significant dopant species present. Silicon and germanium are the most widely used intrinsic semiconductors.
