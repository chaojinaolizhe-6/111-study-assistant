### 2.2.1 Phasor representation of sine wave

### Question:

When we have two sinusoidal AC current with the same frequency, different phase and different amplitude, how to find the total current?

$$
i _ {1} = 1 2. 7 \sqrt {2} \sin (3 1 4 t + 3 0 ^ {\circ}) \mathrm{A}
$$

$$
i _ {2} = 1 1 \sqrt {2} \sin (3 1 4 t - 6 0 ^ {\circ}) \mathrm{A}
$$

（此位置版面为图，见整页扫描图）

$$
i = i_1 + i_2
$$
