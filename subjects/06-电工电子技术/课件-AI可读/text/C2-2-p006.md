### ❺ About the impedance triangle

$$
\varphi = \psi_ {u} - \psi_ {i}
$$

= arctan (X / R)

（此位置版面为图，见整页扫描图）

The total impedance of an RLC series circuit can be found using impedance triangle. First of all, we have to consider the resistance and reactance of each component then put them into the impedance triangle to find the total impedance of the RLC circuit.
