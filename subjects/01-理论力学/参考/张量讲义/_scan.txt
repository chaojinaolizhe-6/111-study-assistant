The Poor Man’s Introduction to Tensors
Justin C. Feng
Center for Relativity
University of Texas, Austin
Abstract
When  solving  physical  problems,  one  must  often  choose  between  writing  formulas  in  a  coordinate
independent form or a form in which calculations are transparent. Tensors are important because they
allow  one  to  write  formulas  in  a  manner  that  is  both  coordinate  independent  and  transparent  in
performing  calculations.  In  these  notes,  I  provide  an  informal  introduction  to  tensors  (in  Euclidean
space) for those who are familiar with the basics of linear algebra and vector calculus.
