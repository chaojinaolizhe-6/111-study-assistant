I work with architect Nathalie Pozzi to do large-scale museum installations, and I also write books about game design, and I teach at the NYU Game Center at Tisch School of the Arts, where we think about games as an art form and also play as a deep form of literacy. Today, I want to talk to you, from my point of view as a game designer, about how to really make something that engages and grabs and hooks an audience, right? I want to finish just with a few quick examples. I was at a dinner party recently, like, a big fancy dinner party, room full of interesting people.
