### ◎Resistors connected in series

$$

$$

$$
I = \frac{U}{R_1 + R_2}
$$

$$
U _ {R _ {1}} = \frac {R _ {1}}{R _ {1} + R _ {2}} U
$$

$$
U _ {R _ {2}} = \frac {R _ {2}}{R _ {1} + R _ {2}} U
$$

◎Impedances connected in series

$$
Z = Z_1 + Z_2
$$

$$
\dot {U} _ {Z_ {1}} = \frac {Z _ {1}}{Z _ {1} + Z _ {2}} \dot {U}
$$

$$
\dot{I} = \frac{\dot{U}}{Z_1 + Z_2}
$$

$$
